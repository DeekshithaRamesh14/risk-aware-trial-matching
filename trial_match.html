Architecture

3-Stage Pipeline

Each stage filters and scores candidates — from 11,601 trials down to the top 20 risk-adjusted matches.

01
Dense Retrieval
PubMedBERT + FAISS IVFFlat
Patient summary encoded into 768-dim biomedical embedding. FAISS approximate nearest neighbour search over all indexed trial criteria returns top 100 candidates.
11,601 → 100
02
Cross-Encoder Reranking
MiniLM-L12-v2
Each candidate is scored jointly with the patient query — capturing nuanced eligibility criteria alignment beyond simple embedding similarity. Top 20 retained.
100 → 20
03
Risk Scoring
XGBoost · ROC-AUC 0.83
XGBoost classifier trained on HINT benchmark predicts trial failure probability using 8 structured features. SHAP values explain each prediction. Final score penalises high-risk trials.
20 → Ranked
Ranking Formula

How Scores Are Computed

A highly relevant but high-risk trial scores lower than a moderately relevant but low-risk one.

FinalScore = NormRelevance × (1 − FailureRisk)
NormRelevance — cross-encoder score normalised to [0,1] FailureRisk — XGBoost predicted failure probability
